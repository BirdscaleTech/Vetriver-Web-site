The Origin

Why Tamil Siddha Scholars Called It the Universal Stabilizer

Chrysopogon zizanioides — known in Tamil as Vettiver — has been cultivated and prescribed by Tamil Siddha healers for over four millennia. Their texts describe it as the plant that "stabilizes the three humors," the Siddha framework for complete physical and mental balance.

Unlike any other natural material, Vetiver roots do not dry out, rot, or lose efficacy. They continue to release therapeutic compounds for 17 to 20 years when simply hung in a space. No electricity, no filter changes, no maintenance.

"The earth has given us one plant that cools the body, purifies the air, calms the mind, and heals the lungs — all at once. That plant is Vettiver."
Tamil Siddha Medical Text, translated from 18th century manuscript

The Farmers

Grown in Three Districts of Tamil Nadu

Vetriver sources exclusively from small-scale farmers in Villupuram, Dindigul and Salem. No middlemen. No industrial processing. Every root is traceable to a family farm.

Villupuram District
North Tamil Nadu
The red-clay soils here produce Vetiver roots with the highest essential oil density. Farmers have cultivated this crop for generations, using zero chemical inputs.
Dindigul District
Central Tamil Nadu
Rocky highland soils force Vetiver roots to grow deeper, concentrating more therapeutic compounds. Dindigul roots are known for their distinctive cooling potency.
Salem District
Western Tamil Nadu
Salem farmers specialize in the longer-root varieties used for Nilavu Mala. Their harvesting process is entirely manual, preserving the root structure that makes the Mala last decades.
The Artisans

Every Mala Woven by a Woman at Home

Each Vetiver Mala is handwoven by women artisans in their homes across Villupuram and surrounding districts. This is not a factory. There are no machines.

A single artisan weaves 8 to 12 Mala per day. The work provides flexible, dignified income for rural women who might otherwise have no employment options. Vetriver is directly responsible for livelihoods across more than 40 homes.

When you buy a Vetiver Mala, you are directly supporting this network. No middleman. The artisan receives a fair wage for every piece.
