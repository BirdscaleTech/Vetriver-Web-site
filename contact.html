Quick Answers
How long does delivery take?
Standard delivery takes 5 to 8 business days across India. Express shipping is available at checkout for 2 to 3 day delivery in major cities.
Can I return or exchange my Mala?
Yes. We accept returns within 7 days of delivery if the product is unused and in original packaging. Exchange is available for any size within 14 days.
Do you offer Cash on Delivery?
Yes, COD is available on orders up to Rs 2,000. For larger orders, prepayment via UPI, net banking, or card is required.
Do you ship outside India?
Currently we ship within India only. International shipping is being set up for 2026. Contact us on WhatsApp for bulk international enquiries.
How do I use the Vetiver Mala?
Hang the Mala near your bed, window, or door. Lightly mist with water on dry days. No electricity or maintenance required. Most customers feel the difference within the first night.
